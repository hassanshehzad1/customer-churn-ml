# Phase 3: Exploratory Data Analysis — Complete Summary

## Maqsad kya tha?
Model banane se pehle data ko "samajhna" — patterns dhoondhna, data ki shape dekhna, aur jo mila usay likh kar record karna, bina kisi data ko modify kiye.

## Humne kya banaya?
Ek notebook: `notebooks/03_eda.ipynb`, jisme 6 hisse hain:

| Hissa | Kya dekha | Kyun zaroori |
|---|---|---|
| Target distribution | Churn: 26.54% Yes, baaqi No | Dataset imbalanced hai — ye aage metrics choose karne mein kaam aayega |
| Numeric histograms | `tenure`, `MonthlyCharges`, `TotalCharges` ki shape | Outliers/anomalies dhoondhna, distribution samajhna |
| Boxplots (numeric vs Churn) | Churners ka tenure kam hai, charges zyada | Pehli dafa pata chala kaunse numeric features signal de rahe hain |
| Count plots (categorical vs Churn) | Contract, InternetService, PaymentMethod, SeniorCitizen | Business-level insights, strong categorical predictors milay |
| Correlation heatmap | tenure-TotalCharges: 0.83 | Multicollinearity ka pata chala |
| Observations (likhi hui) | 5 findings + causation warning | Insights ko permanent record banana |

## Sabse important findings (yaad rakhne wali)

1. **Contract** sabse strong predictor hai: Month-to-month = 42.71% churn, Two-year = sirf 2.83%
2. **Tenure** — naye customers zyada churn karte hain
3. **InternetService** — Fiber optic walay (41.89%) DSL walon (18.96%) se double churn karte hain
4. **MonthlyCharges** — zyada bill, zyada churn
5. **PaymentMethod** — Electronic check walay sabse zyada churn karte hain (45.29%)
6. **SeniorCitizen** — seniors double churn karte hain non-seniors se
7. **tenure aur TotalCharges** 0.83 correlated hain — ye do columns taqriban ek hi information de rahe hain

## Sabse zaroori sabaq jo humne seekha

**Correlation causation nahi hai.** Humne har pattern ko "juda hua lagta hai" bola, "wajah hai" nahi bola — kyunke ye sab patterns aapas mein bhi juday ho sakte hain (jaise naye + fiber optic + electronic check wala ek hi tarah ka customer ho sakta hai). Asal "kaunsa feature sach mein important hai" ye hum **Phase 6** mein model se pata lagayenge (feature importance ke zariye).
