## Ek Misaal: Cricket Match ka Scorebook

Socho aap ek cricket team ke coach hain. Aap ne is saal **bohat saare practice matches** khele:
- Match 1: Team A ke sath, score 150
- Match 2: Team B ke sath, score 180
- Match 3: Team C ke sath, score 165

**Agar aap koi scorebook na rakhein**, to 3 mahine baad aap bhool jayenge "Match 2 mein humne kitna score kiya tha, aur kis strategy se?"

**Agar aap ek scorebook rakhein**, jisme har match ka score, strategy, aur result likha ho, to aap **kabhi bhi** dekh sakte hain "kaunsi strategy sab se behtar thi."

**MLflow wahi scorebook hai, lekin ML experiments ke liye.**

## Humare Project Mein Ye "Matches" Kaunse Hain?

Yaad hai Phase 5, 6, 7 mein humne alag alag **tareeqe** try kiye the model ko behtar banane ke liye? Wo humare "practice matches" the:

| Experiment | Kya Try Kiya |
|---|---|
| Experiment A | Logistic Regression, normal tareeqa, threshold 0.5 |
| Experiment B | Logistic Regression, class_weight laga kar, threshold 0.5 |
| Experiment C | Logistic Regression, class_weight laga kar, threshold 0.6 |
| **Final** | Logistic Regression, normal tareeqa, **threshold 0.3** (jo humne chuna) |

Un sab ke results humne **chat mein tables** mein dekhe, lekin **koi permanent register mein nahi** likhe.

## Part 1 Mein Hum Kya Karne Wale Hain

**Sirf ek kaam:** In 4 experiments ko **MLflow ke scorebook mein likhna**.

Ye 3 chhote steps mein hoga:

### Step 1: MLflow install karna
Ye ek chhoti si library hai (jaise `pandas` ya `scikit-learn`). Install hone ke baad hum isay Python code mein istemal kar sakte hain.

### Step 2: Ek script likhna jo 4 experiments "likh" de
Devin ek chhoti script banayega. Ye script har experiment ke liye ye 3 cheezein MLflow ko batayegi:
- **Kya settings thin** (jaise "class_weight = balanced", "threshold = 0.6")
- **Kya result aaya** (Precision, Recall, F1)
- **Kaunsa experiment humne final chuna** (uspar ek khaas "FINAL" ka label lagana)

**Dhyan dein:** Hum **model dobara train nahi kar rahe** naye tareeqe se. Hum sirf un purane experiments ko **register mein likh rahe hain**, jaise aap purane matches ka score ek naye scorebook mein copy kar dein.

### Step 3: Browser mein scorebook dekhna
Script chalane ke baad, ek command (`mlflow ui`) chalate hain. Ye aapke computer par ek **chhoti website** kholta hai (jaise `http://127.0.0.1:5000`). Us website par aapko ek **table** nazar aayegi:

```
Experiment                         | Precision | Recall | F1
-----------------------------------|-----------|--------|------
Baseline (threshold 0.5)           | 65.8%     | 54.9%  | 59.8%
class_weight (threshold 0.5)       | 52.3%     | 80.5%  | 63.4%
class_weight (threshold 0.6)       | 60.5%     | 72.2%  | 65.9%
FINAL (threshold 0.3)              | 51.9%     | 75.4%  | 61.5%
```

Aap sab experiments **ek hi nazar mein compare** kar sakenge, aur kisi par bhi click kar ke uski tafseel dekh sakenge.

## Ye Kyun Kar Rahe Hain? (Asal Faida)

Abhi humare experiments **chat ki history mein** hain. Agar aap ye chat band kar den, ya 6 mahine baad project dekhen, to **ye sab results kho jayenge**.

MLflow mein likhne se:
1. Results **hamesha ke liye saved** ho jate hain (aapke computer par ek folder mein)
2. Koi bhi (aap, ya interviewer) **ek table mein sab dekh** sakta hai
3. Aap interview mein **screen share kar ke** dikha sakte hain "ye mere experiments thay, aur ye mera final choice"
